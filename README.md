# AthleteOS — Taekwondo Edition V2

**AthleteOS** is a dedicated digital platform for Taekwondo athletes, coaches, academies, and teams, built by **Nova Code**.

## What AthleteOS Does

- Athlete dashboard for keeping important sports information in one place
- Athlete profiles and competition records
- Training plans and training tracking
- Attendance and progress tracking
- Tournament discovery and tournament information updates
- Match, medal, and achievement tracking
- Competition checklists to help athletes prepare
- Weight tracking
- Secure document and certificate management
- AI Coach for training and competition guidance
- Student verification and athlete support features
- Academy and coach features for managing athletes and training
- Notifications and important team updates
- Feedback and support system
- Athlete roadmap and upcoming feature updates
- Responsive experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile

## Built For Athletes

AthleteOS is designed to bring the important parts of an athlete's journey together instead of spreading them across different apps, chats, notes, and documents.

The goal is simple: **train better, prepare smarter, compete with confidence, and keep your athletic journey organized.**
